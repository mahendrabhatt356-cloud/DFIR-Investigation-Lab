# 🚨 Incident Summary — The Midnight Login

## 1. What Happened?

NovaTech Pvt. Ltd. ke security monitoring system ne **02:13 AM** par ek unusual login detect kiya.

Login ek employee account se hua tha.

Problem ye thi ki employee ka normal working time daytime tha, jabki ye login raat me hua.

Security team ne is activity ko immediately suspicious maana, lekin abhi ye confirm nahi tha ki account compromise hua hai.

---

## 2. Initial Alert

**Alert:** Unusual Login

**Time:** 02:13 AM

**User:** `arjun.mehta`

**Department:** Finance

**System:** `NOVA-FIN-07`

**Location:** Remote

**Status:** Under Investigation

---

## 3. Why Was It Suspicious?

Security team ko teen cheezein unusual lagi:

- Login normal working hours ke bahar hua.
- Account finance department ka tha.
- Login ek remote location se hua.

Lekin sirf in points ke basis par ye kehna possible nahi hai ki account hacked tha.

---

## 4. Investigation Questions

The investigation will answer:

### Question 1
Kya `arjun.mehta` ne khud login kiya tha?

### Question 2
Kya is account se pehle ya baad me koi unusual login hua?

### Question 3
Kya login activity ke saath koi aur suspicious event connected hai?

### Question 4
Kya available evidence account compromise ki possibility support karta hai?

---

At the beginning of the investigation, we have:

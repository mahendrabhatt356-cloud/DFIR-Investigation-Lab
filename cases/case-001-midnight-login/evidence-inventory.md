# 📂 Evidence Inventory — Case-001

## 🎬 Why Do We Need Evidence?

A suspicious login alone does not tell us what actually happened.

Before making any conclusion, the investigation team needs to identify and examine the available evidence.

## 🔎 Evidence E-001 — Authentication Logs

The authentication logs show the following pattern:

- Normal office logins were recorded during the day.
- A successful remote login occurred at **02:13 AM**.
- Two failed login attempts followed shortly after.
- A successful office login was recorded the next morning.

### Initial Observation

This pattern is unusual enough to investigate further.

However:

> **An unusual login does not automatically prove unauthorized access.**
